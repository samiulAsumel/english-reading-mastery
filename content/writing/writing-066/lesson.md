## Introduction

[Lesson 66](/lessons/66/) taught you to preserve an author's exact
level of uncertainty — never silently upgrading "may improve" into
"will improve." This task asks you to write your own risk or forecast
statement, calibrated precisely, without either overstating or
underselling your confidence.

## The Rule, for Writing

::: concept
Pick your hedge word deliberately: "may" or "could" for a real
possibility, "likely" for something you expect but can't promise,
"expected to" for a stated projection. Separate the *probability* of a
risk from its *impact* — a low-probability, high-impact risk is not the
same statement as a high-probability, low-impact one.
:::

::: example
*Delays are unlikely under normal conditions, but if a key supplier
fails to deliver on time, the impact on the full schedule could be
severe. The project is expected to finish on schedule, though this
assumes no further disruption.*
:::

## Your Turn

::: prompt words="80-120 words" time="20 min"
Write a short risk or forecast statement for a real or realistic
project or system. Use at least two different hedge words (may, could,
likely, expected to) correctly, and separate the probability of one
risk from its potential impact.
:::

::: draft placeholder="X is [likely/expected/possible] to... / If [condition], the impact could be... / This assumes..."
:::

## Vocabulary in Context

::: vocabulary word="likely" pos="adjective" register="neutral" meaning="probable, though not certain (সম্ভাব্য)" collocation="highly likely, likely to, most likely" example="A delay is likely if the storm continues."
:::

::: vocabulary word="impact" pos="noun" register="formal" meaning="the size or seriousness of an effect, separate from how probable it is (প্রভাব)" collocation="significant impact, impact on, potential impact" example="The impact of a fuel spill would be severe even though the probability is low."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
Under normal conditions, a delay in customs clearance is unlikely — the
process has run smoothly for the past six months. However, if a
document is flagged for manual review, the impact could be significant,
potentially adding several days to the schedule. The shipment is
expected to clear on time, though this assumes no unusual inspection
requirements arise, which remains outside the exporter's control.
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Did you use at least two different hedge words correctly, matching their real strength?
- Did you keep probability and impact as two separate statements, not collapsed into one?
- Did you avoid silently upgrading any hedge into a guarantee anywhere in the passage?
- Did you write directly in English, without composing the passage in Bangla first?
:::
