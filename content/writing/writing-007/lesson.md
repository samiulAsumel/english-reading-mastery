## Introduction

[Lesson 7](/lessons/7/) taught you to read a preposition as an arrow
connecting two ideas, rather than a single word with a fixed Bangla
translation. Writing with that same awareness means choosing the
preposition that builds the exact relationship you mean — not just the
one that sounds close.

## Choosing the Right Arrow

::: concept
**of** connects a whole to a part or an owner: "the opinions **of** the
team." **by** names who or what performs a passive action: "the report
was written **by** the manager." Many verbs and adjectives are fixed to
one particular preposition, and swapping it can flip the meaning: "the
delay resulted **in** a loss" (delay caused the loss) versus "the loss
resulted **from** a delay" (delay is the cause, stated after the
result).
:::

## Your Turn

::: prompt words="4 sentences" time="12 min"
Write four sentences about your work: one using *of* to show a
part-whole or belonging relationship, one passive sentence using *by* to
name who did the action, and two more using fixed pairs from this list:
*depend on, responsible for, interested in, result in, result from*.
:::

::: draft placeholder="The schedule of... / The report was reviewed by... / The delay depends on... / The team is responsible for..."
:::

## Vocabulary in Context

::: vocabulary word="agent" pos="noun" register="technical" meaning="the person or thing that performs an action in a passive sentence (কর্তা)" collocation="the agent of the action, name the agent, omit the agent" example="In 'The container was inspected by customs', 'customs' is the agent."
:::

::: vocabulary word="fixed pair" pos="noun" register="course term" meaning="a verb or adjective that is conventionally followed by one specific preposition (নির্দিষ্ট শব্দগুচ্ছ)" collocation="learn a fixed pair, common fixed pair, fixed pair such as" example="'Responsible for' is a fixed pair: writing 'responsible of' would be an error."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
The schedule of tomorrow's arrivals is already confirmed.

The final report was reviewed by the operations manager.

Our delivery time depends on the weather at the port.

The logistics team is responsible for tracking every container.
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Does your "of" sentence genuinely show belonging or a part-whole relationship, not just proximity?
- In your passive sentence, does "by" correctly name who or what performed the action?
- Did you use at least two fixed verb/adjective + preposition pairs correctly?
:::
