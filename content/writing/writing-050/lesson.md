## Introduction

[Lesson 50](/lessons/50/) taught you to recognize the pattern
"Although X, Y does not necessarily…" instantly — concession,
qualification, main claim — reading the English structure directly into
meaning. This task asks you to build that same three-part sentence
yourself, straight in English, as the capstone of everything this module
has covered.

## The Rule, for Writing

::: concept
A concession sentence has three moving parts: "Although [something
true]," then the main claim, then, often, a qualification that stops the
claim from overreaching — "does not necessarily mean…" This is a single
sentence doing three jobs at once: being fair to the other side,
stating your real point, and not overclaiming.
:::

::: example
"Although the new terminal reduced average dwell time, this does not
necessarily mean overall port efficiency improved, since congestion at
the older terminals stayed the same." Concession (dwell time dropped) →
claim (efficiency conclusion doesn't automatically follow) →
qualification (because congestion elsewhere didn't change).
:::

## Your Turn

::: prompt words="3 sentences" time="15 min"
Write three "Although…" sentences about topics you know well — your
work, your studies, current events, or daily life. Each sentence needs
all three parts: a concession, a main claim, and a qualification that
limits how far the claim goes. Compose them directly in English — don't
write the sentence in Bangla first and translate it.
:::

::: draft placeholder="Although [concession], [claim], since/because [qualification]."
:::

## Vocabulary in Context

::: vocabulary word="concede" pos="verb" meaning="to admit that something is true, often before making a different point — স্বীকার করা" example="The sentence concedes that dwell time dropped before making its real claim."
:::

::: vocabulary word="overreach" pos="verb" meaning="to claim more than the evidence actually supports — অতিরিক্ত দাবি করা" example="Adding a qualification stops the claim from overreaching."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
Although the company hired ten new engineers this year, this does not
necessarily mean the product will ship faster, since the new hires are
still being onboarded and won't be fully productive for months. Although
sales grew by 15 percent last quarter, this does not necessarily mean the
new marketing campaign worked, since the growth started before the
campaign launched. Although the exam results improved this term, this
does not necessarily mean the new teaching method is better, since this
term's exam was also easier than last term's.
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Does every sentence contain all three parts — concession, claim, qualification?
- Does the qualification genuinely limit the claim, rather than just repeating the concession?
- Did you write these directly in English, without composing them in Bangla first?
:::
