## Introduction

[Lesson 22](/lessons/22/) taught you to decode dense phrases like *the
ability of employees to adapt* by finding the head noun first. This task
runs the process backward: you start with a plain idea and build the
dense phrase yourself — exactly the skill a professional email, report,
or CV needs when three short sentences would read as amateur where one
compressed sentence reads as confident and precise.

## The Rule, for Writing

::: concept
Pick a head noun that names the idea (*ability, extent, development,
tendency, failure*), attach *of + who/what* to it, then *to + verb* to
name the action. **The ability of the new system to process large
orders quickly** compresses three separate facts — there's a new
system, it processes large orders, and it does so quickly — into one
noun phrase built around a single head.
:::

## Your Turn

::: prompt words="1 sentence" time="10 min"
Here are three plain sentences: *The port installed a new scanning
system. The system checks containers faster than before. This has
reduced how long trucks wait at the gate.* Combine all three ideas into
ONE professional sentence, using at least one pattern from Lesson 22
(such as "the ability of X to Y," "the development of X," or "X has
reduced the extent to which Y").
:::

::: draft placeholder="The [head noun] of the new scanning system to ... / The development of ... has reduced ..."
:::

## Vocabulary in Context

::: vocabulary word="reduce" pos="verb" register="neutral" meaning="to make something smaller in amount or degree (কমানো)" collocation="reduce costs, reduce risk, significantly reduce" example="A second gate lane reduced waiting time by half."
:::

::: vocabulary word="compress" pos="verb" register="formal" meaning="to fit a larger amount of meaning into fewer words (সংকুচিত করা)" collocation="compress a sentence, compress the schedule, heavily compress" example="Writers compress long explanations into a single precise sentence."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
The port's new scanning system has reduced the extent to which trucks
wait at the gate by processing containers faster than before. (Equally
valid: "The ability of the new scanning system to process containers
faster than before has significantly reduced truck waiting times at the
gate.")
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Did you find one head noun and build the sentence around it, rather than joining three sentences with "and"?
- Does your of-phrase and to-phrase (if used) attach in the right order, exactly as Lesson 22 modelled?
- Does the sentence sound like natural professional English when read aloud, not like a translated pattern?
- Did every idea from the original three sentences survive the compression?
:::
