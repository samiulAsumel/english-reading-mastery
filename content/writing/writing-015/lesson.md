## Introduction

[Lesson 15](/lessons/15/) taught you to expand a compact phrase like "the
data collected from participants" back into its full "that were
collected" form. This task runs the process the other way: you start
with two full sentences and compress the second one down into a short
participle phrase, exactly the way a real writer would.

## The Rule, for Writing

::: concept
Find the noun your second sentence is really describing, then drop
"who/that + be" and keep only the verb form. If the noun performs the
action, use V-ing (active). If the noun receives the action, use V3
(passive). Then attach the participle phrase directly next to the noun
it describes — placing it next to the wrong noun creates a dangling
modifier, a sentence that accidentally claims the wrong thing did the
action.
:::

::: example
**Two sentences:** The inspector reviewed the report. The report was
submitted late.
**Compressed:** The inspector reviewed the report submitted late.
("submitted late" describes "report" — passive, V3.)

**Two sentences:** The workers operate the crane. The workers arrived
early this morning.
**Compressed:** The workers operating the crane arrived early this
morning. ("operating the crane" describes "workers" — active, V-ing.)
:::

## Your Turn

::: prompt words="2 sentences" time="10 min"
Here are two pairs of plain sentences. Pair 1: *The driver delivered the
package. The driver knows this route well.* Pair 2: *The engine was
repaired last week. The engine now runs smoothly.* Compress each pair
into ONE sentence by turning the second sentence into a participle
phrase attached to the correct noun.
:::

::: draft placeholder="Pair 1: The driver ...ing... / Pair 2: The engine ...ed... now runs smoothly"
:::

## Vocabulary in Context

::: vocabulary word="submit" pos="verb" register="formal" meaning="to formally hand in or present something, such as a document (জমা দেওয়া)" collocation="submit a report, submit for approval, submit by the deadline" example="Applicants must submit the form by Friday."
:::

::: vocabulary word="route" pos="noun" register="neutral" meaning="the path taken between one place and another (রুট/পথ)" collocation="shipping route, alternative route, on route" example="The carrier chose a longer route to avoid the storm."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
**Pair 1:** The driver delivering the package knows this route well.
**Pair 2:** The engine repaired last week now runs smoothly.
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Did you choose V-ing for an active relationship and V3 for a passive one?
- Is your participle phrase placed right next to the exact noun it describes?
- Could a reader mentally expand your compressed sentence back to "who/that + be + verb" and recover the same meaning?
- Did you write both sentences directly in English, without translating from Bangla first?
:::
