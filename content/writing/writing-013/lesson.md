## Introduction

[Lesson 13](/lessons/13/) taught you to read a passive sentence and ask
why the author put a particular noun at the front. Writing one is the
reverse move: you already know both the event and the two ways to tell
it — now you choose, on purpose, which one to write.

## The Rule, for Writing

::: concept
Start from **Subject + be + V3**. If your sentence's real point is *who
did it*, write active: *Doer + verb + receiver.* If your sentence's real
point is *what happened to something*, write passive: *Receiver + be +
V3 (+ by + doer)*. Add "by + doer" only when naming the doer actually
helps the reader — drop it when the doer is obvious, unimportant, or
simply less interesting than the result.
:::

::: example
**Active, doer in focus:** The terminal staff inspected every container
before loading.
**Passive, receiver in focus:** Every container was inspected before
loading.
Both sentences are true. The first is about what the staff did; the
second is about what happened to the containers — and dropping "by the
terminal staff" was a choice, not an accident.
:::

## Your Turn

::: prompt words="2 sentences" time="10 min"
Here is one event: *A technician replaced the faulty sensor on Line 3
this morning.* Write it as ONE active sentence with the technician in
focus. Then write it as ONE passive sentence with the sensor in focus —
decide yourself whether to keep "by the technician" or drop it, and be
ready to explain why.
:::

::: draft placeholder="Active: The technician... / Passive: The faulty sensor..."
:::

## Vocabulary in Context

::: vocabulary word="inspect" pos="verb" meaning="to look at something carefully to check its condition — পরিদর্শন করা" example="Every container was inspected before it left the terminal."
:::

::: vocabulary word="faulty" pos="adjective" meaning="not working correctly, because of a defect — ত্রুটিপূর্ণ" example="The faulty sensor was replaced before the line restarted."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
**Active:** The technician replaced the faulty sensor on Line 3 this
morning.
**Passive:** The faulty sensor on Line 3 was replaced this morning. ("By
the technician" is dropped here — once the sensor is fixed, who exactly
replaced it matters far less than the fact that Line 3 is running
again.)
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Does your active sentence open with the doer, and your passive sentence open with the receiver?
- Did your passive sentence use the correct "be + V3" form for its tense?
- Can you explain, in one sentence, why you kept or dropped "by the technician"?
- Did you write both sentences directly in English, without composing them in Bangla first?
:::
