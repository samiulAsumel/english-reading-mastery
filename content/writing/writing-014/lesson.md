## Introduction

[Lesson 14](/lessons/14/) taught you to look at *reading, to understand,*
and *by practicing* and instantly know what job each one is doing.
Producing these shapes yourself is the next step — and it's where the
gerund-versus-infinitive choice stops being abstract and starts being a
decision you make, sentence by sentence.

## The Rule, for Writing

::: concept
Three shapes to build on purpose: a **gerund** (verb+ing) standing as
your subject when you're naming an activity itself; a **verb +
infinitive** (want to, decide to, need to) when you're completing a
verb's meaning or stating a goal; and **preposition + gerund** (by,
without, after + V-ing) when you're naming a method. Never put a bare
base verb after a preposition — that slot always takes the -ing form.
:::

::: example
**Gerund as subject:** Checking the manifest twice prevents costly
loading errors.
**Verb + infinitive:** The clerk needs to confirm the container count
before departure.
**Preposition + gerund:** You can avoid delays by confirming the
manifest early.
:::

## Your Turn

::: prompt words="3 sentences" time="10 min"
Write three separate sentences about a task you do regularly (at work,
at home, or while studying): one using a gerund phrase as the subject,
one using a verb + infinitive pattern, and one using "by" or "without" +
a gerund to describe a method.
:::

::: draft placeholder="Gerund subject: ...ing... / Verb + infinitive: I need to... / By + gerund: ...by ...ing"
:::

## Vocabulary in Context

::: vocabulary word="manifest" pos="noun" meaning="an official list of a ship's or aircraft's cargo — মালপত্রের তালিকা" example="Checking the manifest twice prevents costly loading errors."
:::

::: vocabulary word="confirm" pos="verb" meaning="to state that something is definitely true or correct — নিশ্চিত করা" example="The clerk needs to confirm the container count before departure."
:::

::: vocabulary word="delay" pos="noun" meaning="a period of time by which something is made later than expected — বিলম্ব" example="You can avoid delays by confirming the manifest early."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
**Gerund as subject:** Reviewing my notes every evening helps me
remember new vocabulary.
**Verb + infinitive:** I decided to study for thirty minutes every
morning before work.
**Preposition + gerund:** I improved my speaking without translating a
single sentence into Bangla.
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Does your gerund sentence use the -ing form as the subject, not as part of a continuous verb?
- Does your verb + infinitive sentence use "to + base verb," not "to + -ing"?
- Does the word right after "by" or "without" take the -ing form, never the base verb?
- Did you write all three sentences directly in English, without translating from Bangla first?
:::
