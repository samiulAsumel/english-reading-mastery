## Introduction

[Lesson 18](/lessons/18/) taught you to recover the hidden verb inside a
noun like "implementation." This drill tests whether you can keep up
with that density live — shadowing a formal sentence at full speed, then
saying its plain-English meaning immediately, before you have time to
write anything down.

## Shadow the Model

::: shadow rate="0.9"
The introduction of the new scheduling system led to a reduction in
missed deliveries.
:::

Listen once, all the way through, without trying to decode it yet. Then
shadow it, keeping the same pace through the whole subject — don't slow
down or pause in the middle of "the introduction of the new scheduling
system."

## Your Turn

::: prompt time="10s"
Once you've shadowed the sentence above, say its plain-English meaning
out loud in one sentence — find the hidden verb first ("introduce"),
then say what it means in ordinary words. Start the moment the timer
begins.
:::

::: timer seconds="10" label="Say the plain-English meaning now"
:::

## Record Yourself

::: record label="Shadow the sentence, then say its plain-English meaning, and record both."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
Something close to: "The company introduced a new scheduling system, and
fewer deliveries have been missed since." There's no single correct
wording — what matters is that you found the hidden verb (introduce),
spoke a complete sentence, and didn't just repeat the original words
back unchanged.
:::

## Vocabulary in Context

::: vocabulary word="scheduling" pos="noun" register="formal" meaning="the process of planning when things will happen (সময়সূচি নির্ধারণ)" collocation="scheduling conflict, scheduling of, scheduling system" example="A scheduling conflict caused two vessels to arrive for the same berth."
:::

::: vocabulary word="missed" pos="adjective" register="neutral" meaning="failed to happen, arrive, or be caught at the intended time (মিস হওয়া/হাতছাড়া)" collocation="missed deadline, missed connection, missed opportunity" example="A missed connection at the transhipment port added four days to the journey."
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Did you shadow the full sentence without slowing down mid-phrase?
- Did you find the hidden verb before trying to paraphrase?
- Did your spoken paraphrase come out as one fluent sentence, not a word list?
:::
