## Introduction

[Lesson 13](/lessons/13/) taught you to notice why a writer opens a
sentence with the receiver of an action instead of the doer. This drill
asks you to build that same choice live — describing a process step out
loud, in passive voice, with no time to plan the sentence first.

## Shadow the Model

::: shadow rate="0.9"
The containers are scanned before they're loaded onto the vessel.
:::

Listen once all the way through, then shadow it. Notice "are scanned"
carries the sentence's main stress, not "containers" — in fluent speech
the passive verb itself is never rushed or swallowed.

## Your Turn

::: prompt time="10s"
Scenario: at a warehouse, staff weigh every package before it ships.
When the timer starts, say one passive sentence describing this step,
with "package" or "every package" opening the sentence — speak
immediately, no planning pause.
:::

::: timer seconds="10" label="Speak your passive sentence now"
:::

## Record Yourself

::: record label="Say your passive sentence, then record it and compare against the model."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
There's no single correct sentence — "Every package is weighed before it
ships" and "Packages are weighed before shipping" are both natural. What
matters is that the passive verb ("is weighed" / "are weighed") came out
immediately and correctly, with the package, not the staff, opening the
sentence.
:::

## Vocabulary in Context

::: vocabulary word="weigh" pos="verb" meaning="to measure how heavy something is — ওজন করা" example="Every package is weighed before it ships."
:::

::: vocabulary word="ship" pos="verb" meaning="to send goods somewhere, usually by a carrier — পাঠানো/চালান করা" example="Packages are weighed before shipping."
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Did you speak immediately, with no pause to plan the sentence?
- Did your passive verb use the correct "be + V3" form?
- Did the receiver of the action, not the doer, open your sentence?
:::
