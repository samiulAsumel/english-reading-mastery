## Introduction

This lesson doesn't introduce a new skill. It asks you to do everything
from the last five lessons in one sitting: name something instantly,
shadow a native rhythm, and answer a scenario on the spot, with nothing
to translate from at any point. If you can get through this drill
without a long silent pause, you've built the core habit this whole
module was built around — and Level 2's speaking module starts from
exactly this baseline, with longer, more complex scenarios.

## Round 1 — Instant Naming

::: prompt time="8s"
Look around you and name three different objects, one at a time,
restarting the timer for each.
:::

::: timer seconds="8" label="Name it — restart for each of the three objects"
:::

## Round 2 — Shadow for Rhythm

::: shadow rate="0.85"
I'm going to describe my day. This morning, I woke up early. Then I had
breakfast. After that, I came to work.
:::

## Round 3 — Spontaneous Scenario

::: prompt time="20s"
A stranger at a bus stop asks: "So, what do you do?" Start the timer and
answer out loud as if they were really standing in front of you —
include your job, one thing you like about it, and one detail about your
day so far, all in one continuous answer.
:::

::: timer seconds="20" label="Answer the stranger — keep talking the whole time"
:::

## Record Yourself

::: record label="Record Round 3 — your full spontaneous answer — and play it back."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
"I work as a port operations assistant, actually. I really enjoy it
because every day is a little different — one day it's vessel
schedules, the next it's coordinating with the shipping agents. Today's
been fairly calm so far, which has been a nice change." Notice the
shape, not the exact words: job, opinion about it, one real detail —
said as one continuous answer, not three separate memorized sentences.
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Did Round 1's naming come out faster than it did in Speaking 1?
- Did your Round 2 shadowing keep pace with the model without lagging?
- In Round 3, did you speak for the full 20 seconds as one continuous answer, with no long pause to translate?
- Compared to Speaking 1, does your overall pace feel noticeably closer to natural speech?
:::

This closes the Foundation speaking module. Level 2's speaking module
starts from this same baseline — longer scenarios, real conversational
exchanges, and less time on each timer.
