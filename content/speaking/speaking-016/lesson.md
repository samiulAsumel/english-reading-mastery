## Introduction

[Lesson 16](/lessons/16/) taught you to separate a claim from its source
and its reporting verb. This drill asks you to build that same sentence
live — naming a source, choosing a reporting verb, and stating the
claim, all in one breath, the instant the timer starts.

## Shadow the Model

::: shadow rate="0.9"
Recent studies suggest that regular sleep improves memory.
:::

Listen once all the way through, then shadow it. Notice "suggest"
carries real stress here, not a throwaway word — it's the piece of the
sentence that tells the listener exactly how confident the claim is.

## Your Turn

::: prompt time="10s"
Scenario: a colleague tells you the new software "argues that manual
data entry causes most errors." When the timer starts, say one sentence
attributing this claim, using "argue" as your reporting verb — speak
immediately, no planning pause.
:::

::: timer seconds="10" label="Speak your attributed claim now"
:::

## Record Yourself

::: record label="Say your attributed claim, then record it and compare against the model."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
Something close to: "The report argues that manual data entry causes
most errors." There's room for wording, but the sentence must follow
source + reporting verb + that + claim, spoken as one fluent unit.
:::

## Vocabulary in Context

::: vocabulary word="entry" pos="noun" register="neutral" meaning="an item of information entered into a record or system (এন্ট্রি/তথ্য লিপিবদ্ধকরণ)" collocation="make an entry, log entry, data entry" example="The clerk corrected the entry in the cargo register."
:::

::: vocabulary word="colleague" pos="noun" register="neutral" meaning="a person you work with (সহকর্মী)" collocation="a colleague from, work with colleagues, senior colleague" example="My colleague says the entry in the system was wrong."
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Did you speak the source, reporting verb, and claim in the correct order?
- Did you speak immediately, with no pause to plan the sentence?
- Did your reporting verb match the strength the scenario actually described?
:::
