## Introduction

Every real conversation — a job interview, meeting someone new, a
casual chat — opens with a small set of predictable questions: What do
you do? Where are you from? Why did you choose this? These are exactly
the questions where a translation habit shows up most painfully, because
there's a real person waiting for your answer, not a blank page. This
lesson drills those questions directly, under time pressure, so the
answers start coming without a visible pause to think.

## Your Turn

::: prompt time="6s"
For each question below, start the timer and answer out loud
immediately, in a full sentence, before it reaches zero: "What do you
do?" / "Where are you from?" / "Why did you start learning English?" /
"What do you do on weekends?" / "How long have you worked at your
current job?"
:::

::: timer seconds="6" label="Answer now — full sentence, before the timer ends"
:::

## Shadow the Model

::: shadow rate="0.85"
I work as a port operations assistant. I'm from Chattogram. I started
learning English to work internationally. On weekends, I usually spend
time with my family.
:::

## Record Yourself

::: record label="Answer all five questions out loud in sequence, then play yourself back."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
"I work as a [your role]." "I'm from [your city]." "I started learning
English because [your real reason]." "On weekends, I usually [a real
weekend activity]." "I've worked here for [a length of time]." There's
no fixed correct wording — the goal is a complete, true sentence
produced within the six seconds, not a specific answer.
:::

## Vocabulary in Context

::: vocabulary word="hesitation" pos="noun" meaning="a pause caused by uncertainty, before speaking or acting — দ্বিধা" example="Notice any hesitation before your answer and try to shorten it."
:::

::: vocabulary word="recover" pos="verb" meaning="to return to normal after a mistake or interruption — পুনরুদ্ধার করা" example="If you start a sentence wrong, recover by finishing it a different way instead of stopping."
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Did you answer in a full sentence, not just a single word, for each question?
- Did you answer within the six-second window for at least 4 of the 5 questions?
- If an answer came out wrong or incomplete, did you keep going in English rather than switching to Bangla to think?
:::
