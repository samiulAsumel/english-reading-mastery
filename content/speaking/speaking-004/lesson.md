## Introduction

This lesson combines the last three: naming things (Speaking 1), naming
actions (Speaking 2), and natural rhythm (Speaking 3). The task is
simple to state and genuinely demanding to do: describe what's around
you, out loud, for thirty seconds, without stopping. Thirty seconds of
continuous speech, with no script, is one of the clearest tests of
whether you're speaking directly in English or still running a
translation loop — a translation loop breaks down fast under continuous
pressure; direct English speech doesn't.

## Your Turn

::: prompt time="30s"
Look around you. When you start the timer, describe out loud everything
you can see and everyone's doing, in full sentences, for the entire
thirty seconds. If you run out of things to say, describe the same thing
a different way rather than stopping. Don't pause to translate — if a
word won't come, describe around it and keep going.
:::

::: timer seconds="30" label="Describe out loud — keep talking the whole time"
:::

## Shadow the Model

::: shadow rate="0.85"
I am sitting at my desk. There is a window on my left. Outside, people
are walking on the street. A car is parked near the gate. It is quiet in
here right now.
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
I am sitting in my office right now. There is a computer in front of me
and a cup of tea next to it. Outside the window, a few people are
walking past. Someone is talking on the phone in the next room. It is a
quiet, ordinary afternoon, and I am focusing on this exercise.
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Did you keep talking for the full 30 seconds, without a long silent pause?
- When you couldn't find a word, did you describe around it instead of stopping?
- Listening back, how many real pauses did you count — and were they shorter than before?
:::
