## Introduction

[Lesson 15](/lessons/15/) taught you to mentally expand a compressed
phrase like "the workers operating the crane" back to its full form.
This drill asks you to build that same compression live — hearing two
ideas and saying them as one sentence, with a participle phrase doing
the work of a whole clause.

## Shadow the Model

::: shadow rate="0.9"
The technician repairing the engine finished before noon.
:::

Listen once all the way through, then shadow it. Notice "repairing the
engine" is spoken as one smooth unit, at the same pace as the rest of
the sentence — a participle phrase never gets a pause on either side of
it.

## Your Turn

::: prompt time="12s"
Two ideas: the manager sent the report; the manager arrived late today.
When the timer starts, say ONE sentence combining both, using a
participle phrase to compress the first idea onto "manager" — speak
immediately, no planning pause.
:::

::: timer seconds="12" label="Speak your combined sentence now"
:::

## Record Yourself

::: record label="Say your combined sentence, then record it and compare against the model."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
Something close to: "The manager sending the report arrived late today."
There's some room for wording, but the participle phrase must attach to
"manager" and use the active V-ing form, since the manager is doing the
sending.
:::

## Vocabulary in Context

::: vocabulary word="repair" pos="verb" register="neutral" meaning="to fix something that is broken or damaged (মেরামত করা)" collocation="repair a fault, urgent repair, repair work" example="The crane needs repair before it can lift heavy loads again."
:::

::: vocabulary word="noon" pos="noun" register="neutral" meaning="twelve o'clock in the middle of the day (দুপুর ১২টা)" collocation="at noon, by noon, before noon" example="The vessel is due to berth before noon."
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Did you shadow the model sentence without pausing around the participle phrase?
- Did your combined sentence attach the participle phrase to the correct noun?
- Did you speak immediately, with no pause to plan the sentence first?
:::
