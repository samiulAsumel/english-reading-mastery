## Introduction

[Lesson 41](/lessons/41/) taught you to unpack a dense sentence in
writing, with time to think. Explaining something out loud, on the spot,
is the real test — a colleague asks "wait, what does that mean?" and you
have seconds, not minutes, to answer accurately.

## Shadow the Model

::: shadow rate="0.9"
The company's implementation of a redesigned system appears to have
resulted in a reduction in processing errors.
:::

Listen once, then shadow it at full density. Then say the plain version
out loud yourself: "The company changed its system, and errors seem to
have gone down since then" — notice "appears to" survives as "seem to."

## Your Turn

::: prompt time="15s"
You'll hear a dense sentence with a hedge word in it. In 15 seconds,
explain its meaning out loud in plain English — keep the hedge, don't
drop it into a flat certainty.
:::

::: timer seconds="15" label="Explain it in plain English now"
:::

## Record Yourself

::: record label="Explain the sentence, then record it and check whether your hedge matched the original's caution."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
Given *"the organization's adoption of automated scheduling appears to
have resulted in a modest reduction in overtime"*: *"The organization
started using automated scheduling, and overtime seems to have dropped
a bit since then — though it's not certain the scheduling change is
what actually caused it."* "Appears to" and "modest" both survive as
"seems to" and "a bit" — nothing got stated more confidently than the
original allowed.
:::

## Vocabulary in Context

::: vocabulary word="hedge" pos="noun" register="technical" meaning="a word that softens a claim's certainty (সতর্কতাসূচক শব্দ)" collocation="use a hedge, hedge such as, soften with a hedge" example="'Roughly' and 'appears to' are hedges that soften a spoken claim."
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Did your spoken explanation keep the same level of certainty as the original sentence?
- Did you explain in genuinely plain words, not just repeat the original phrasing slightly reworded?
- Did you speak fluently within the time limit, without long pauses?
:::
