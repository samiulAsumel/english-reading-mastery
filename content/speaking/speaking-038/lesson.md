## Introduction

[Lesson 38](/lessons/38/) taught you to read connectors as precise
logical signals. Speaking under time pressure makes this harder, not
easier — there's no time to browse a list of synonyms, so you have to
reach for the right one instantly.

## Shadow the Model

::: shadow rate="0.9"
The system is fast. Nevertheless, the company decided to slow the
rollout, judging the risk too high to move quickly.
:::

Listen once, then shadow it. Notice how "nevertheless" gets slightly
more stress and a slightly longer pause before it than a plain "but"
would — the extra weight matches the extra logical work it's doing.

## Your Turn

::: prompt time="12s"
You'll hear two related facts: a system saves money, but it requires
retraining staff. In 12 seconds, say one sentence joining them with the
connector that best fits a concession — the company adopted it anyway —
not just a plain contrast.
:::

::: timer seconds="12" label="Speak your connected sentence now"
:::

## Record Yourself

::: record label="Say your sentence, then record it and check which connector you actually reached for."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
*"The system saves money; nevertheless, it requires significant staff
retraining, and the company decided to adopt it anyway."* A plain
"however" would only mark the contrast; "nevertheless" is stronger here
because the sentence also reports that the company acted despite the
difficulty — concession plus persistence, not just contrast.
:::

## Vocabulary in Context

::: vocabulary word="concession" pos="noun" meaning="acknowledging a point against your position before continuing to hold it — ছাড়/স্বীকৃতি" example="Saying 'nevertheless' out loud signals concession plus persistence."
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Did you choose a connector that matches concession-plus-persistence, not just plain contrast?
- Did you speak the full sentence within the time limit, with only a natural pause before the connector?
- Would a listener understand exactly how the two facts relate, without you explaining further?
:::
