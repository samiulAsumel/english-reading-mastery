## Introduction

Here's the actual mechanism behind "translating in your head before you
speak": your brain forms the idea, converts it to a Bangla sentence,
then translates that sentence into English, then finally says it. Three
steps where there should be one. The only way to remove the middle two
steps is to practice situations where there's no time for them — and no
Bangla sentence to begin with, because the prompt never gives you one.

That's this whole drill. Look at something. Name it in English.
Immediately. The timer isn't decoration — it's the entire point.

## Your Turn

::: prompt time="8s"
Look around wherever you are right now. Pick one object you can see.
When you start the timer, say its English name out loud before it
reaches zero. Do this five times, picking a different object each time.
:::

::: timer seconds="8" label="Say the word — don't translate first"
:::

## Shadow the Model

::: shadow rate="0.85"
A window. A chair. A table. A phone. A door.
:::

Play it once, listening only. Play it again, saying each word half a
second behind the recording — that's shadowing, and it trains your mouth
to move at native speed instead of your own, slower, translated speed.

## Record Yourself

::: record label="Say five object names out loud, then play yourself back."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
Any correct English noun for something actually in front of you counts
as correct here — there's no single right answer. What matters is
whether you said it before the timer ended, and whether you said it
without first forming the word in Bangla.
:::

## Vocabulary in Context

::: vocabulary word="object" pos="noun" meaning="a physical thing you can see or touch — বস্তু" example="Name five objects in this room."
:::

::: vocabulary word="immediately" pos="adverb" meaning="right away, with no delay — তাৎক্ষণিকভাবে" example="Say the word immediately, before you think about it."
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Did you say the word before the timer reached zero, for at least 3 of your 5 tries?
- Did you notice yourself forming a Bangla word first, even once? (Noticing it is the first step to stopping it.)
- When you shadowed the model, did your pace get closer to the recording on the second try?
:::
