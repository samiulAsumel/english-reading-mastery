## Introduction

[Lesson 1](/lessons/1/) taught you that the smallest complete English
sentence is just Subject + Verb: *People think. Birds fly.* Reading that
pattern and producing it out loud, under time pressure, are two
different skills. This drill trains the second one — no time to build a
Bangla sentence first, because the prompt never gives you one.

## Shadow the Model

::: shadow rate="0.9"
People walk. The phone rings. Water boils. Children play. The door
opens.
:::

Listen once, all the way through, without speaking. Then play it again
and say each one half a second behind the recording — notice that every
sentence is only two words, said quickly, with no pause between Subject
and Verb.

## Your Turn

::: prompt time="6s"
Look around you and pick something that is doing something — a person
walking, a fan turning, a phone ringing. When the timer starts, say one
Subject + Verb sentence about it out loud, immediately. Do this five
times with five different things.
:::

::: timer seconds="6" label="Say it now — Subject, then Verb, nothing else"
:::

## Record Yourself

::: record label="Say five Subject + Verb sentences out loud, then play yourself back and compare your pace to the shadowed model."
:::

## Model Answer

::: model-answer
Any true Subject + Verb sentence about something actually around you
counts — "The fan turns," "My phone rings," "The rain falls." There is
no single correct answer; what matters is that you said it before the
timer ended and without composing a Bangla sentence first.
:::

## Vocabulary in Context

::: vocabulary word="ring" pos="verb" register="neutral" meaning="to make a clear ringing sound (বাজা)" collocation="the phone rings, ring loudly, keeps ringing" example="The phone in the gate office rings whenever a truck arrives without a booking."
:::

::: vocabulary word="turn" pos="verb" register="neutral" meaning="to move in a circle or change direction (ঘোরা)" collocation="turn left, turn around, turn towards" example="The trucks turn left at the scale house and queue for the inspection lane."
:::

## Self-Check

::: rubric
- Did you say a complete Subject + Verb sentence before the timer reached zero, for at least 3 of your 5 tries?
- Did you notice yourself forming a Bangla sentence first, even once?
- Did your shadowed pace get closer to the recording on your second attempt?
:::
